How it stays up to date

  1. It refreshes itself: new postings are pulled in daily, so the list is always current.
  2. Filter: by type (PSW, behavioural, DSW, clerical, restorative, coordination) and location.
  3. Apply: open any card and use its application link.
  4. Tailor: tap "Show tailored resume" and "Show cover letter". A resume and cover letter built for that posting appear. Then download them as Word files.
What's in every tailored resume and cover letter
  • Summary rewritten with the posting's keywords
  • Most relevant bullets moved to the top
  • Matching skills kept; extra section added for the job type (e.g. Administration for clerical roles)
  • Single column, no tables or images. It parses perfectly in ATS software.
  • 100% true to your experience. Never fabricated.
  • A cover letter with your 4 strongest points, in simple English, ready to send
